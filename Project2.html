About Her Ground Foundation

Sole engineer and lead designer for the foundation's website and brand presence — from custom calendar logic to content strategy across Instagram and Discord. This project spans the full stack of building a nonprofit's public identity, not just the code.

Why I Start?

A small foundation needed a real public presence — a site to explain its mission, a way for people to find and join events, and a consistent voice across social platforms — but no budget for a full team. I took this on to give the founder one person who could own the whole picture: code, design, and content, working together instead of disconnected.

Problem Statement and Solution

Problem Statement

  • No unified website to represent the foundation's mission.
  • No easy way for the community to see or join events.
  • Inconsistent voice across Instagram and Discord.
  • No dedicated team to build or maintain a site.

Solution

  • Designed and built a full multi-page site solo.
  • Built a custom calendar with create/edit/delete logic.
  • Aligned content strategy across platforms with the founder.
  • Added async form states for a smoother user experience.

Highlights

  • Built a client-side event calendar with create/edit/delete functionality and custom date-navigation logic, no external library
  • Implemented async form handling with loading/error/success states for smoother user feedback
  • Designed a responsive multi-page site with shared components and scroll-triggered animations
  • Partner with the founder on content strategy and maintain brand consistency across platforms
